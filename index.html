🧠 What is typed-lm?

typed-lm is a powerful tool that lets you turn large AI language models like Llama, Qwen, Mistral, or Gemma into a quick decision-making assistant. Think of it as a translator between the AI's understanding and the specific answers you need.

Instead of getting long paragraphs of text, you get clear, structured decisions. It's perfect for sorting customer feedback, categorizing documents, identifying spam, or any task where you need a fast, accurate yes/no or category answer from an AI.

🌟 Why Choose typed-lm?

  • ⚡ Incredibly Fast: Makes decisions in a single pass, giving you answers at lightning speed.
  • 🔧 Advanced Training: Uses LoRA and QLoRA techniques to customize the AI to understand your specific needs without starting from scratch.
  • 💾 Efficient Storage: Includes FP8 and FP4 quantization to make the AI files smaller and faster, saving you space.
  • 🦀 Built with Rust: Known for reliability and speed, so you know your program is running efficiently.
  • 📦 Works with Popular Models: Ready to use with the most common AI models available today.
  • 🌐 Web-Friendly: Built on Actix-web, so it can easily connect to web applications.

⚙️ How Does It Work?

Imagine you need to categorize thousands of customer comments. Instead of reading each one, typed-lm takes your AI model and gives it a special "decision maker" on top. It presents the AI with your categories, and the AI quickly tells you which category each comment belongs to.

The program also lets you retrain the AI on your specific examples using LoRA, so the AI gets better at your exact type of classification over time.

🔍 Feature Highlights

🎯 Semantic Routing

Understands the meaning behind your text, not just keywords. It makes smarter choices about what category your input fits into.

Teaches the AI model to specialize in your domain. Add your own examples and it gets better at the specific task you need it to do.

💽 FP8/FP4 Quantization

Makes the AI models smaller and faster to run without sacrificing much accuracy. This means less memory usage and quicker responses.
